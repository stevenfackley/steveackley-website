Client work: an authenticated, workflow-heavy operations app rather than a content site. ASP.NET Core Identity with SMS and email OTP, QuestPDF and OpenXML document generation, EF Core on PostgreSQL, shipped as Docker Compose on EC2 behind a Cloudflare Tunnel. The repository is private; the app is live at p1opshub.steveackley.org.
